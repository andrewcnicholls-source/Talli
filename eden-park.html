Eden Park Guide

What to know before you walk in.

Walking time from 86 Paice Ave

Around 1.6km, roughly a 20–22 minute walk to the Eden Park gates. Allow a bit longer once crowds build up close to kick-off.

Before You Go

Good to check on the official site

Rules can change event to event, so it's always worth a quick check before you head in.

Bag policy

Eden Park allows small bags and soft chilly bins within set size limits, but restricts large bags, coolers and certain other items. Limits vary by event, so check the current conditions of entry for your specific event.

Gates, seating & stadium map

For gate opening times, your seating area and a map of the stadium, the official Eden Park event page for your match or show is the best source.

Eden Park official site →

On the day

Aim to arrive with plenty of time before gates close, especially for big fixtures. A liquor ban applies in the neighbourhood around the stadium on event days, and re-entry once you've left is not always allowed — check your ticket type.
