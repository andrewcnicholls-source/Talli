Eden Park Guide

What to know before you walk in.

Walking time from 86 Paice Ave

750m | 8-minute walk to the Eden Park gates. Make sure to allow some time to get to your seat.

Before You Go

Entering Eden Park

Rules can change event to event, so it's always worth a quick check before you head in.

Bag policy

Eden Park allows small bags and soft chilly bins within set size limits, but restricts large bags, coolers and certain other items. Limits vary by event, so check the current conditions of entry for your specific event.

Gates, seating & stadium map

From Talli, head straight up Sandringham Road towards the stadium. You will be approaching Eden Park from the South-West side.

Depending on where your tickets are located, it's best to check which gate you need before you arrive.
