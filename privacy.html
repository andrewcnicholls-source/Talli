The Legal Stuff

Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and who else ever sees it.

1. Who we are

Talli Parking (Talli Limited) runs event parking at 86 Paice Ave, Sandringham, Auckland. We are the agency responsible for the personal information described here under the Privacy Act 2020.

Anything on this page, ask us about it: talli.parking@gmail.com.

2. What we collect when you book

Only what it takes to hold a space for you and find your car on the night:

  • Your email address. Required — it is where your confirmation goes and how we reach you if the event moves.
  • Your name and phone number, if you give them. Both are optional.
  • Your licence plate, if you give it. It is how the marshal ticks you off at the gate without asking your name in the dark.
  • What your vehicle needs — its size, and whether it is low to the ground — so we put you somewhere it fits.
  • Whether you agreed to a spot on the street or berm, where the option was offered.
  • Your booking itself — which event, which type of spot, your arrival window, what you paid, and whether you arrived.

If you buy at the gate rather than online, we record the same booking details, and your name, phone and plate only if you offer them.

3. Your card details never reach us

Payment is taken by Stripe, on Stripe's own checkout page. Your card number is entered there and is never sent to us, never passes through this website, and is not stored anywhere in our systems.

What comes back to us is the outcome: that the payment succeeded, the amount, and Stripe's reference for it, so a receipt, a refund or a dispute months later still points at the right booking.

4. Registering interest in an event

Some fixtures are announced before they go on sale. If you ask to be told when one opens, we keep your email address, and your name if you gave it, against that event.

We use it to send you one message: that the event is on sale. It is not a general mailing list, we do not sell it, and you can ask us to take you off it at any time.

5. No tracking, no analytics, no advertising cookies

This is the short section, and it is short because it is true. This website carries no analytics, no advertising pixels, no social media trackers and no third-party scripts that watch what you do. We do not build a profile of you and we have nothing to share with anyone who would.

Your browser does store a small amount of information for the site to work — remembering that you got past the practice site's front door, or keeping a staff member signed in to the gate screen. None of it identifies you to us or leaves your device for that purpose.

6. Why we hold it

To take the booking and confirm it. To know which cars are expected on the night and place them. To contact you if the event is cancelled, moved, or something goes wrong with your spot. To process refunds and answer disputes. And to keep the business records that tax law requires us to keep.

That is the whole list. We do not use your details for anything you have not asked for.

7. Who else sees it

Four companies, each doing one job, none of them buying or selling your information:

  • Stripe takes the payment and holds the card details we never see. Governed by Stripe's own privacy policy.
  • Supabase hosts the database your booking is stored in.
  • Cloudflare serves this website.
  • Google signs in the people who run the gate screen. This one does not touch customers at all — it verifies a staff email address, nothing more.

Beyond those, we disclose personal information only where the law requires it, or where we need to in order to establish or defend a legal claim.

8. Where it is stored

Our database is hosted in Sydney, Australia. Stripe and Cloudflare are international companies and process information on servers overseas, including outside Australia and New Zealand.

We are telling you this because the Privacy Act 2020 says you should know when your information leaves the country. Each of these providers is bound by privacy obligations comparable to New Zealand's.

9. How long we keep it

Booking and payment records are kept for seven years. That is not our preference — it is how long New Zealand tax law requires business records to be retained.

If you registered interest in an event and never booked, we clear that once the event has been and gone.

10. Keeping it safe

The database cannot be read from a web browser. Bookings are written only by our own server-side code, never directly from this page. The gate screen — the one that shows the night's arrivals — requires a Google sign-in tied to an account we have specifically approved, checked on every single request.

No system is perfect, and we would rather say that than claim otherwise. If something ever went wrong with your information, we would tell you.

11. Seeing it, and correcting it

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is wrong. Email talli.parking@gmail.com and we will come back to you within 20 working days.

You can also ask us to delete what we hold, and we will, except where we are required to keep it — see section 9.

If you are not happy with how we have handled it, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how any of this works, we will change this page and update the date below. There is no archive of old versions; what is here is what applies.
