The Legal Stuff

Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and who else sees it.

What we collect

We collect personal information from you, including information about your:

  • name
  • contact information
  • licence plate

Why we collect it

We collect your personal information in order to:

  • provide payment confirmation
  • allow parking hosts to manage their parks

Who we share it with

Besides our staff, we share this information with:

  • hosts, in order to allow them to track parking at their property.

Hosts sign in with Google

The people who run a car park sign in to our booking system with their Google account. All we receive from Google is the email address that identifies them, and we use it for nothing else. This does not apply to customers, who never sign in.

Some of it is optional

Providing some information is optional. If you choose not to enter contact details, we'll be unable to confirm bookings.

Seeing it, and correcting it

You have the right to ask for a copy of any personal information we hold about you, and to ask for it to be corrected if you think it is wrong.

If you'd like to ask for a copy of your information, or to have it corrected, please contact us at talli.parking@gmail.com, or 021 347 028, or 86 Paice Avenue.

Changes to this policy

If we change how any of this works, we will change this page and update the date below.
