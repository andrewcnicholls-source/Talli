The Legal Stuff

Terms & Conditions

By entering and parking at 86 Paice Ave, you agree to these terms.

1. Use at Owner's Risk

Vehicles and their contents are left entirely at the owner's risk. Talli Parking accepts no liability for any loss, theft, or damage to any vehicle, its contents, or any personal property while on the premises.

2. No Security Guarantee

While we manage the parking space efficiently during events, no active security, surveillance, or continuous monitoring of individual vehicles is provided or guaranteed.

3. Parking Instructions

You must follow all instructions given by our team regarding where and how to park. We often utilize double parking to maximize space. If we do require your keys to move your vehicle deeper into the lot, your vehicle is still left at your own risk.

4. Your Vehicle

Your vehicle must be road legal — a current warrant of fitness and a current vehicle licence (registration).

If any fine, infringement or other charge arises because a vehicle is not road legal, it is the responsibility of the person responsible for that vehicle, not Talli Parking.

5. Booking & Payment

Spots booked through this website are paid for at the time of booking, by card. Your booking is confirmed once payment succeeds — you'll get a receipt by email and a confirmation page showing your reference, your arrival window and where to go.

You can also turn up on the night without booking, subject to space. In that case payment is due on arrival, by cash or bank transfer, and unpaid vehicles may be asked to leave.

6. Arrival Windows

Each spot comes with an arrival window, shown when you book. Please arrive within it — we hold your specific space until the window closes, after which we may re-let it and place you wherever there is room.

Nobody is asked to leave an event early. However, we park cars in stacks, so some spots sit in front of another car. If yours is one of them, please come back to it promptly once the event finishes so the person behind you can get out. Where this applies it is stated clearly before you pay and shown on your confirmation.

7. Cancellations & Refunds

Cancel at least 24 hours before the event starts and we'll refund you in full. Email talli.parking@gmail.com with your booking reference or license plate.

If the event is cancelled, postponed or rescheduled, you get a full refund or a transfer to another date — your choice.

If we can't provide the spot you paid for, you get a full refund, whether or not you've already arrived.

Cancellations inside 24 hours, and no-shows, are not refunded — the space has been held for you and can't be resold that late. If something has gone wrong, get in touch anyway and we'll be reasonable about it.

Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 or the Fair Trading Act 1986.

8. Abandoned Vehicles

Please return to your vehicle promptly following the conclusion of your event. Vehicles left significantly after the event finishes without prior arrangement may be towed at the owner's expense.
